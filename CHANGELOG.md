# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First release.

- **The installer opens the port by itself:** it always opens TCP 4789 in Windows Firewall for every network type (home Wi-Fi is often set to Public), allows VBuddy's own node.exe, and removes any earlier Block rule for it. The firewall tick box is gone.
- **Two editions of the installer:** `pnpm package:both` makes `VBuddy-Setup-<version>.exe` (with the Cloud relay switch for use away from home) and `VBuddy-Local-Setup-<version>.exe` (home network edition: no relay at all, the switch is hidden, the PC never connects to any VBuddy server, phones connect on the same Wi-Fi). Use `pnpm package:local` for the home edition alone. Installing one over the other switches editions and keeps your flights and settings.
- **Fenix EFB, full screen:** the full-screen EFB page has a "‹ VBuddy" button at the top left that returns to the app.

- **Fenix EFB inside VBuddy, at home and away:** Systems has a new EFB tab (and the PC window an EFB page) that shows the Fenix external EFB from your PC. VBuddy passes the EFB through its own address, so it works on your phone over the cloud relay as well, not only on your home network. It opens only on the PC and on paired devices. The EFB is looked for on port 8083 (change with `--efb-port` or `VBUDDY_EFB_PORT`). To use it away from home, update the relay on your cloud VM (the relay now also carries POST requests and binary data for the EFB; run deploy/relay/setup.sh again with the new relay build). Not yet tried against the real Fenix EFB, so some of its pages may need follow-up fixes.

- **New VBuddy window on the PC:** a sidebar with Connect (addresses, pairing code, cloud relay, paired devices), Live map, Flights, Systems, Atlas, Activity and Settings. Live map, Flights, Systems and Atlas are the app itself, inside the window. Activity lists what the bridge has been doing (with a problems-only filter). Settings has: reconnect to MSFS, pause the simulator later (at a time, or before top of descent, held on the PC), start VBuddy when you sign in to Windows, install or update the vPilot plugin, and your SimBrief and Hoppie details. The window opens larger (1180 x 780). Traffic planes on the map are bigger.

- **My flights and Atlas:** plan the flights you want to fly (from, to, aircraft, date, notes; change or delete them later, kept on your PC). Every flown flight now shows where it left from and arrived at, how far it went and how long it took; tap one to see its details and its route drawn on the map. Atlas totals your flights, hours, distance, airports and countries, lists the airports you have visited and the aircraft you fly most. Airports come from the bundled OurAirports list (public domain). Only flights flown on the route of a filled-in SimBrief plan are logged in the Atlas (the plan's departure and arrival must match where you actually took off and landed); other flights stay in My flights, marked as not logged. A flight's distance is worked out when it ends and kept, so totals stay right after the detailed route is cleared (telemetry is still kept for 24 hours by default).

- **Systems page reads every aircraft the way Cobalt does:** the same simulator variables, read in three groups (engines every few frames, autopilot, fuel, radios and the rest a few times a second, doors, warnings and weather once a second). The main engine gauge follows the engine type (N1 for jets, torque and propeller RPM for turboprops, RPM for pistons). Lights that only show in the aircraft's "lights on" states (many Airbus) now count, the squawk is decoded correctly, and the Fenix and FlyByWire read their FCU targets, managed dots, mode lights and switches from their own cockpit variables. New Overview with instruments and flight phase.

- **New app layout:** a full-screen map with a draggable bottom sheet and a floating tab bar (Flights, Systems, Inbox, Atlas, Search), a bridge status pill, round map buttons (appearance, traffic, ATC sectors, follow) and a settings button. On a tablet or PC the sheet is a side panel. Same burgundy look, light and dark.

- **Systems tab:** a live page of the aircraft: engines (N1, N2, EGT, fuel flow, oil, vibration), fuel, autopilot, surfaces, radios and squawk, electrical and hydraulics, pressurization, doors, lights, warnings and the weather around you. Read once a second from the simulator; values an aircraft does not report show a dash.

- **New look:** burgundy brand theme with **light and dark mode** (button in the top bar: same as device / light / dark; the map follows it). Flight tab redesigned around a hero card (altitude, airspeed, vertical speed, ground speed, heading dial); bottom tab bar on phones, side menu on tablets and desktops; Manrope and Barlow Condensed fonts are bundled, so it looks the same offline.

- **Brief tab:** your SimBrief flight plan (route, time, fuel, weights) and, for the departure, arrival and alternate airports, the current METAR (decoded: wind, visibility, cloud, temperature, QNH, VFR/MVFR/IFR/LIFR) and TAF. Look up any airport by ICAO code. Weather is cached for 5 minutes. Enter your SimBrief name or pilot ID, and (for the coming Hoppie messaging) your Hoppie logon code, in the **VBuddy window** on the PC; they are saved on the PC only and never go to the relay or a phone.

- **ACARS (Hoppie)** in the Comms tab: send telex, answer CPDLC messages (WILCO / UNABLE / STANDBY, AFFIRM / NEGATIVE, ROGER), request a pre-departure clearance filled in from your SimBrief plan, request METAR / TAF / ATIS, and request a CPDLC logon. The bridge checks for new messages about once a minute while the simulator is connected, and a new message is pushed to your devices if Messages notifications are on. Needs your Hoppie logon code (VBuddy window, Your accounts).
- **Fix (2):** the Fenix's AP1/AP2 lights now also switch the autopilot flag in the live data on the bridge, so every device shows "Autopilot on" without depending on the Cockpit data reaching the app.
- **Discovery tool:** type a label (for example `DIR`) and press Enter, then press that key in the cockpit; the report lists what changed under each label. For finding MCDU keys later.
- **Fix:** the Flight tab showed "Autopilot off" on the Fenix. It now also reads the Fenix's AP1/AP2 lights, shows an Autothrust chip, and takes the selected speed, heading, altitude and vertical speed from the Fenix FCU.
- **Simulator and systems card:** pause/resume, gear, spoilers, parking brake, engines, APU, pitot heat, seat belts, flaps, time rate, altimeter and camera, each checked against the simulator. **Pause later** at a set time or before the top of descent, kept on the PC.
- **iFly buttons and knobs:** buttons are now pressed and released (a momentary press), and a knob first tries the aircraft's own "set" control before stepping it up or down, with wider name matching.
- **PMDG 737 / 777 switched on:** driven from the aircraft's own SDK data (found in its package folder, or copied into the `sdk` folder). PMDG liveries titled without the maker name (for example "737-800 PAX BW SC") are now recognised. The discovery tool skips GSX and camera-tool variables and has `--lvar-filter`.
- **Autopilot controls for add-on aircraft:** the Cockpit tab now shows an autopilot panel for every aircraft, not only the Fenix. PMDG 737 knobs use PMDG's own SDK events, FlyByWire A32NX / A380X use its FCU events, and iFly 737 MAX uses its own input events. Each change is checked and reported honestly if the aircraft ignores it. PMDG buttons are still to come.
- **Top of descent notice:** a phone notification (5 to 30 minutes before, default 10; chosen per device) that the descent should start soon, worked out from your SimBrief plan and your ground speed.
- **Autopilot-off notice (optional):** tick "Autopilot switches off in the cruise" in the notification settings to get a phone notification and a banner if the autopilot drops out above 3,000 ft above ground after being on for 30 seconds or more. Off by default; separate from Aircraft alerts.
- **Aircraft alerts** (phone notification and a banner in the open app): engine stopped in flight, stall warning, overspeed, gear up while low and descending, high sink rate below 1,000 ft, and low fuel. The fuel warning time is chosen per device (15 to 90 minutes, or off); it is worked out from how fast the fuel has really been burning over the last few minutes.
- **Notification settings** per device: ATC boundary warning distance (10 to 150 nm), an optional hourly limit on boundary and no-ATC notices ("Contact" calls always come through), and a **VATSIM connected / disconnected** notice.

- **Bridge** for MSFS 2024 (SimConnect) with a built-in test flight; serves the web app on your network. Runs as a notification-area (tray) icon with Open, Copy address, Pairing code, Settings, Log, Start with Windows, Restart and Exit.
- **Flight** dashboard, **Map** with live VATSIM traffic and ATC boundary alerts, **Landings** log with touchdown rate, G, flare and bounces.
- **Cockpit**: Fenix A320 FCU and EFIS with split-touch knobs, lights card for every aircraft. Other Airbus: lights only. Cockpit controls are Airbus-only in this release.
- **Comms**: Zulu clock, VATSIM status, COM1/COM2 tuning and listen toggles, transponder, chat (radio, private, broadcast, SELCAL), unread badge and chime, via the **VBuddy Link** vPilot plugin. "Listen on this device" opens VATSIM Listen.
- **Pairing**: viewing is open on your network; controlling needs a code from the bridge PC.
- **VBuddy window** on the bridge PC (opens from the tray icon): simulator and vPilot status, phone address with QR code, pairing code, paired devices, and the cloud relay switch.
- **Away from home**: a shared cloud relay (https, `vbuddy.duckdns.org`) that anyone can switch on in the VBuddy window with no setup: each install makes its own id and key, so many PCs share one relay and nothing secret is built in. Phone link with QR code. Or host your own. Phone notifications for ATC, landings and chat.
- **Flight log** on disk with automatic catch-up after a dropped connection.
- Windows installer (bundles Node.js), portable zip, optional Start-with-Windows and firewall rule.
- Tools: `aircraft:discover` (find an aircraft's controls), `fenix:discover`, `pmdg:dump`.

Known limits: not yet tested end-to-end against a real MSFS 2024, vPilot or relay VM by the author's automated tests; IVAO is not included; PMDG, iniBuilds, iFly and FSLabs cockpit panels are not available yet (the PMDG code is present but switched off).
